Most permanent exterior signs in Edmonton require a sign permit from the City of Edmonton Development Services. This typically applies to storefront signs, projecting signs, and freestanding signs. Temporary signs and window graphics generally do not require a permit. Permit applications are submitted by the business owner or their agent. We can advise on what is typically required for your project type and help you prepare the necessary artwork and specifications — but the permit application is your responsibility as the building occupant or owner. For official permit information, visit the City of Edmonton Signs page.
